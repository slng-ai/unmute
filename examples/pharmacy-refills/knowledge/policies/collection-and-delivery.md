# Collection and delivery

## Opening hours

The counter is open Monday to Friday, nine in the morning until six in the
evening. It closes for lunch between one and half past one. It is shut on
Saturday, Sunday and bank holidays.

The last collection of the day is taken at ten minutes to six.

## Collecting your own order

Bring nothing. Give your name and the first line of your address at the counter
and the team will find the bag.

We hold a ready order for fourteen days. After that the medicine goes back on
the shelf and the order has to be placed again.

## Collecting for somebody else

You may collect for a relative, a neighbour or a friend. Bring the name and the
first line of their address, and be able to say what the medicine is for the
team to check against the bag.

For a medicine in the controlled list, the rules are stricter and the controlled
medicines document covers them.

## Delivery

Free delivery within three miles of the shop for anyone over sixty five, anyone
registered housebound, and anyone collecting a medicine the counter team has
agreed to deliver.

Deliveries run Tuesday and Thursday afternoons. Ask for delivery when you place
the order, not after, because a bag already put out for collection cannot be
moved to the van the same week.

Somebody has to be in to take the delivery. The driver will not leave medicine
with a neighbour, in a porch or in a bin store. If nobody answers, the bag comes
back to the shop and you can collect it or wait for the next delivery day.

## If your order is not ready

Progress checks are easy to get wrong from the shelf, so ask on the phone rather
than making the trip. The counter can tell you whether the order was received,
whether it has been dispensed, and whether it is waiting on the surgery.
