# Repeat prescriptions at Elmtree Pharmacy

## What a repeat prescription is

A repeat prescription is a medicine a prescriber has already approved for you
more than once. The prescriber sets how many issues you may have before they
look at it again. Each issue is called a repeat.

## Ordering one

Order by phone, at the counter, or through the pharmacy app. To order by phone
you need the prescription reference. It is two letters, four digits and one
letter, printed on the side of the box and on the paper slip that came with your
last order.

If you cannot find the reference, the counter team can find the order from your
surname and date of birth. That takes a little longer because somebody has to
check it by hand.

We order one medicine at a time. If you take three medicines, that is three
references.

## How long it takes

Two working days from the order to the medicine being ready. The counter is shut
on Saturday and Sunday, so an order placed on Friday is ready on Tuesday.

A few medicines are not held in the shop and are brought in overnight. When that
happens the counter team rings you and the order takes one more working day.

## When the repeats have run out

When the last repeat has been used, the pharmacy cannot order the medicine
again. The request goes to the surgery, and the prescriber decides whether to
approve another course. That usually takes three working days, and longer if the
prescriber wants to see you first.

Do not wait until the last box to find out. Order the medicine when about one
week of it is left.

## Changes

The pharmacy cannot change a dose, a quantity or a strength. Only the prescriber
can. If the box you have does not match what you expect, ask to speak to a
pharmacist before you take any more of it.
