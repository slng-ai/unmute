# Golden Wok: allergens and ingredients

This is what the kitchen can say about allergens. If a question is not answered
here, do not guess. Tell the caller that someone will check and ring them back.

## The kitchen is not allergen free

Every dish is cooked in one kitchen with shared woks, shared oil and shared
utensils. We cannot promise that any dish is free of traces of another. A caller
with a severe allergy should be told this plainly before they order.

## Peanuts and tree nuts

Peanuts are used in the satay sauce, the kung pao chicken and the crispy
seaweed. Cashews are used in the chicken with cashew nuts.

The fryer oil is rapeseed oil, not peanut oil.

## Gluten

Our standard soy sauce contains wheat. Any dish with a sauce should be treated
as containing gluten unless it is listed below.

Made without gluten containing ingredients on request: the steamed rice, the
plain boiled noodles are not, and the salt and pepper chicken can be prepared
with a rice flour coating if the caller asks when they order. Say that this
takes about ten minutes longer.

## Shellfish and fish

Prawns appear in the prawn crackers, the prawn toast, the sweet and sour prawns
and the special fried rice. The prawn crackers are fried in the same oil as
everything else.

Oyster sauce is used in the beef in black bean sauce and in the mixed vegetable
chow mein. Fish sauce is used in the Thai style curries.

## Eggs and dairy

Egg is in the egg fried rice, the egg noodles, the prawn toast and the batter
used for the sweet and sour dishes.

We use almost no dairy. There is no butter, cream or cheese in the kitchen.

## Vegetarian and vegan

Dishes marked vegetarian on the menu contain no meat and no fish, but several
contain egg or oyster sauce. A vegan caller should be asked to say so, and the
kitchen will swap the oyster sauce for a mushroom sauce.

## Spice

Mild, medium and hot are the three levels. Hot is genuinely hot. The chilli oil
and the fresh chillies are both added at the end, so a dish can be made milder
if the caller says so when they order.
