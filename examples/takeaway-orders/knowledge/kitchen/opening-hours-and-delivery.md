# Golden Wok: opening hours, collection and delivery

## Opening hours

The kitchen takes orders from midday until half past ten, Tuesday to Sunday.
We are closed all day Monday. The last order of the night goes in at half past
ten and the phone stops taking orders then, even if someone is still eating in
the shop.

On a bank holiday we open at five in the afternoon and close at ten.

## Collection

Collection is the fastest way to get food from us. A normal collection order is
ready in fifteen to twenty minutes. On a Friday or Saturday evening, between
half past six and half past eight, it is closer to thirty five minutes.

Collection orders are paid for at the counter. We take cash and cards. We do not
take payment over the phone.

## Delivery

We deliver within three miles of the shop. The delivery charge is two pounds
fifty and there is a minimum order of fifteen pounds.

Delivery takes forty five minutes to an hour on a normal evening, and up to an
hour and a half on a Friday or Saturday. If the driver cannot find the address
they ring the number on the order, so the caller should give a number they will
answer.

We cannot deliver to an address outside the three mile ring. Those orders have
to be collected.

## Changing or cancelling an order

Once an order is placed it goes straight to the kitchen. A caller who needs to
change or cancel one has to ring the shop back and speak to someone at the
counter. The phone agent cannot do it.
